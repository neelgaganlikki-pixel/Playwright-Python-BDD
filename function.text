  - Feature: Time and Attendance - Weekly Timesheet Lifecycle
  - Tags: @timesheet, @regression
  - Objective: Validates complete weekly timesheet business flow: dynamic employee
               creation with direct supervisor mapping, timesheet logging (40 hrs),
               submission, supervisor rejection with comments, revision of hours,
               supervisor approval, and employee edit lock verification.
  - Step-by-Step Flow:
      1. Given an employee is registered with a reporting supervisor
         * Logs into OrangeHRM as Admin.
         * Extracts Admin user display name from .oxd-userdropdown-name.
         * Navigates to PIM > Add Employee.
         * Fills First Name (e.g. TimeF87656), Last Name, and unique Employee ID.
         * Toggles "Create Login Details" switch.
         * Inputs unique ESS Username and Password (Password123!).
         * Saves employee and captures internal empNumber from URL.
         * Clicks "Report-to" tab and assigns logged-in Admin as "Direct" reporting supervisor.
         * Saves supervisor mapping, records data in datafilled.txt, and logs out Admin.
      2. When the employee logs in and navigates to My Timesheets
         * Logs in with the newly created ESS employee credentials.
         * Navigates to Time > Timesheets > My Timesheets (/time/viewMyTimesheet).
      3. And the employee enters project "Apache" and logs 8 hours from Monday to Friday
         * Clicks "Create Timesheet" or "Edit" button.
         * Types "Apache" into Project autocomplete field and selects matching project.
         * Selects activity from dropdown.
         * Fills 8.00 hours across Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday (40 hrs total).
      4. And saves and submits the timesheet
         * Clicks "Save" button and waits for toast notification.
         * Clicks "Submit" button and waits for confirmation.
      5. Then the timesheet status should display "Submitted"
         * Waits for status subtitle .oxd-text--subtitle-2 to contain "Submitted".
         * Asserts timesheet status contains "Submitted".
      6. When the supervisor logs in and reviews the employee timesheet
         * Logs out employee and logs back in as Admin supervisor.
         * Navigates directly to /time/viewTimesheet/employeeId/{empNumber}.
      7. And rejects the timesheet with comment "Please correct Wednesday hours"
         * Inputs rejection comment "Please correct Wednesday hours" in textarea.
         * Clicks "Reject" button.
         * Waits for success toast notification.
      8. Then the timesheet status should display "Rejected"
         * Waits for status subtitle .oxd-text--subtitle-2 to update to "Rejected".
         * Asserts timesheet status contains "Rejected".
      9. When the employee logs in and views their timesheet
         * Logs out Admin supervisor and logs back in as ESS employee.
         * Navigates to My Timesheets.
     10. Then the timesheet status should display "Rejected"
         * Confirms timesheet status displays "Rejected" on employee portal.
     11. When the employee updates Wednesday hours to 7.00 and re-submits the timesheet
         * Clicks "Edit" button.
         * Locates Wednesday daily hours input (index 2) and updates value to "7.00".
         * Clicks "Save" button.
         * Clicks "Submit" button to re-submit for approval.
     12. Then the timesheet status should display "Submitted"
         * Confirms timesheet status transitions back to "Submitted".
     13. When the supervisor reviews the employee timesheet and clicks approve
         * Logs out employee and logs back in as Admin supervisor.
         * Navigates to employee's timesheet (/time/viewTimesheet/employeeId/{empNumber}).
         * Clicks "Approve" button and awaits success toast.
     14. Then the timesheet status should display "Approved"
         * Waits for status subtitle to transition to "Approved".
         * Asserts timesheet status contains "Approved".
     15. And the timesheet should be locked from further employee edits
         * Logs out Admin and logs back in as ESS employee.
         * Navigates to My Timesheets and waits for status "Approved".
         * Asserts "Edit" button is hidden/disabled (locked from further employee modification).
